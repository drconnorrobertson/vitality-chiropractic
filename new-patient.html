What to Expect at Your First Visit

We want your first visit to be as smooth and comfortable as possible. Here is what you can expect when you come to Vitality Chiropractic & Rehab for the first time.

Before Your Visit

Please arrive 10-15 minutes early to complete any remaining paperwork. You can save time by completing your new patient forms online before your appointment. Be sure to bring:

  • A valid photo ID
  • Your insurance card (if applicable)
  • Any relevant medical records or imaging
  • A list of current medications
  • Comfortable clothing that allows for movement

Your First Appointment

Your initial visit will include:

  • A detailed health history review
  • A thorough physical examination
  • Orthopedic and neurological testing
  • Movement and functional assessment
  • Discussion of findings and treatment recommendations
  • Your first treatment (if appropriate)
