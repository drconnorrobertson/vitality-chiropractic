Our Philosophy

Vitality Chiropractic & Rehab provides the highest quality, professional and compassionate chiropractic care to ensure that you and your family is in good health.

At Vitality Chiropractic & Rehab, we take a sports evidence-based model and apply it with a functional whole body approach. We listen first, then develop personalized treatment plans that address the root cause of your pain and dysfunction.

Our practice is built on the belief that everyone deserves access to high-quality, individualized care. Whether you are an elite athlete recovering from an injury or someone who simply wants to get through the day without pain, we are here to help.

We combine chiropractic adjustments, soft tissue therapy, functional rehabilitation, and corrective exercises to help our patients achieve optimal movement and wellness. Our approach is always conservative, always evidence-based, and always patient-centered.
