Chiropractic Care During Pregnancy and Beyond

Pregnancy brings incredible changes to a woman’s body. As your body adapts to support your growing baby, it is common to experience back pain, pelvic discomfort, sciatica, and other musculoskeletal complaints. Chiropractic care offers a safe, drug-free approach to managing these symptoms and supporting your body through every stage of pregnancy and postpartum recovery.

Prenatal Chiropractic

During pregnancy, hormonal changes cause ligaments to relax, and the shifting center of gravity places new stresses on the spine and pelvis. Prenatal chiropractic adjustments are designed to:

  • Reduce back, neck, and joint pain
  • Maintain proper pelvic alignment for optimal baby positioning
  • Alleviate sciatic nerve pain
  • Support overall comfort throughout pregnancy
  • Help maintain an active lifestyle during pregnancy

Postpartum Chiropractic

After delivery, your body begins the process of recovery. Postpartum chiropractic care helps you recover faster and return to your normal activities by addressing:

  • Pelvic realignment after delivery
  • Upper back and neck pain from nursing and carrying your baby
  • Core stability and strength restoration
  • Diastasis recti management
  • Return to exercise and activity guidance

Why Choose Vitality for Pre/Post Natal Care?

Dr. CJ has specialized training in prenatal and postnatal chiropractic techniques. She uses gentle, safe methods specifically designed for pregnant and postpartum patients. Every treatment plan is individualized to meet you where you are in your journey.
