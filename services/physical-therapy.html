Rehabilitation That Gets Results

Physical therapy at Vitality Chiropractic & Rehab is designed to help you recover from injury, surgery, or chronic conditions. Our evidence-based approach combines hands-on manual therapy with targeted therapeutic exercises to help you return to the activities you love.

Our Approach to Physical Therapy

Every patient receives a thorough evaluation that includes a detailed history, movement assessment, and functional testing. Based on these findings, we develop a customized treatment plan that may include:

  • Manual therapy and joint mobilization
  • Therapeutic exercise prescription
  • Neuromuscular re-education
  • Balance and proprioception training
  • Functional movement training
  • Home exercise programs
  • Injury prevention strategies

Conditions We Treat with Physical Therapy

  • Post-surgical rehabilitation
  • Chronic pain conditions
  • Joint replacements
  • Sprains and strains
  • Tendonitis and tendinopathy
  • Balance disorders
  • Workplace injuries
  • Motor vehicle accident injuries

Why Choose Vitality for Physical Therapy?

At Vitality, our physical therapy programs are integrated with chiropractic care for a comprehensive approach to rehabilitation. Dr. CJ’s background in sports medicine and performance enhancement allows her to design rehabilitation programs that go beyond symptom relief to address the underlying causes of dysfunction.
