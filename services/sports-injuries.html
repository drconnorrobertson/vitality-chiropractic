Expert Sports Injury Care

As a sports chiropractor with a background in sports medicine and performance enhancement, Dr. CJ is uniquely qualified to diagnose, treat, and help prevent sports-related injuries. Whether you are a competitive athlete or a weekend warrior, we provide the care you need to recover and perform at your best.

Common Sports Injuries We Treat

  • Muscle strains and ligament sprains
  • Shoulder injuries (rotator cuff, impingement, labral tears)
  • Knee injuries (ACL, MCL, meniscus, patellofemoral syndrome)
  • Ankle sprains and instability
  • Tennis elbow and golfer’s elbow
  • Shin splints and stress fractures
  • Plantar fasciitis
  • Hip and groin injuries
  • Concussion management
  • Overuse injuries

Our Sports Injury Treatment Approach

We use a combination of techniques to get you back to your sport safely and efficiently:

  • Thorough biomechanical and movement assessment
  • Chiropractic adjustments to restore joint function
  • Soft tissue therapy and myofascial release
  • Instrument-assisted soft tissue mobilization
  • Sport-specific rehabilitation exercises
  • Return-to-sport protocols
  • Injury prevention programs
  • Performance optimization strategies

Return to Sport Safely

Our goal is not just to get you out of pain but to help you return to your sport stronger and more resilient than before. We use progressive return-to-sport protocols that ensure you are ready to perform at your best while minimizing the risk of re-injury.
