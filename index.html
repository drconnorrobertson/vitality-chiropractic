Welcome to Vitality Chiropractic & Rehab

We listen. We take a sports evidence-based model and apply it with a functional whole body approach. Our goal is to get you moving again, whether you are an elite athlete or someone simply looking to get through the day pain-free.

At Vitality Chiropractic & Rehab, we believe in treating the whole person, not just the symptoms. Dr. Caitlin Jordan combines her extensive sports medicine background with chiropractic expertise to deliver personalized care plans that address the root cause of your pain.
