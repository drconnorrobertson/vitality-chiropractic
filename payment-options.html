We believe that everyone deserves access to quality chiropractic care. We offer a variety of payment options and accept most major insurance plans to make your treatment as affordable as possible.

Insurance Verification

We are happy to verify your insurance benefits before your first visit. Simply call our office at (412) 646-1991 and our team will check your coverage and explain any out-of-pocket costs before your appointment.

Co-pays, deductibles, and self-pay fees are due at the time of service. We accept cash, checks, and all major credit cards.
