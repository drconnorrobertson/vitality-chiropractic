Dr. Caitlin Jordan

DC, MS — Sports Chiropractor

Dr. Caitlin Jordan, affectionately known as Dr. CJ, is the founder and lead chiropractor at Vitality Chiropractic & Rehab. With a deep passion for sports medicine and movement, Dr. CJ brings a unique and comprehensive approach to chiropractic care.

Dr. CJ earned her Bachelor of Science in Sports Medicine from the University of Pittsburgh, where she developed a strong foundation in anatomy, biomechanics, and athletic performance. She then pursued a Master of Science in Performance Enhancement and Injury Prevention from California University of Pennsylvania, further specializing in the science of human movement.

She completed her Doctor of Chiropractic degree at New York Chiropractic College, one of the nation’s premier chiropractic institutions. This rigorous training equipped her with the clinical skills and evidence-based approach that define her practice today.

Education

  • BS in Sports Medicine — University of Pittsburgh
  • MS in Performance Enhancement & Injury Prevention — California University of Pennsylvania
  • Doctor of Chiropractic (DC) — New York Chiropractic College

Dr. CJ’s Philosophy

Dr. CJ believes in treating every patient as a whole person, not just a set of symptoms. Her sports evidence-based model ensures that each treatment plan is grounded in the latest research while being tailored to the individual needs of every patient.

Whether you are an elite athlete, a weekend warrior, a new mom, or someone looking to improve your daily quality of life, Dr. CJ is committed to helping you move better and live better.
