What We Offer

From injury recovery to wellness maintenance, we provide a full range of chiropractic and rehabilitation services.

👶

Pre/Post Natal Chiropractic

Safe, gentle chiropractic care for expecting and new mothers throughout pregnancy and postpartum recovery.
⚛

Physical Therapy

Customized rehabilitation programs to restore function, improve mobility, and prevent future injuries.
⚽

Sports Injuries

Expert diagnosis and treatment for all types of sports-related injuries using evidence-based protocols.
🩹

Chiropractic Adjustments

Spinal and extremity adjustments to restore proper joint function, reduce pain, and improve nervous system performance.

💪

Soft Tissue Therapy

Manual soft tissue techniques including myofascial release, instrument-assisted soft tissue mobilization, and trigger point therapy.

🏃

Functional Rehabilitation

Corrective exercise and functional movement training to address underlying movement dysfunctions and prevent re-injury.

🧠

Dry Needling

Targeted trigger point therapy to reduce muscle tension, alleviate pain, and improve range of motion.

📈

Performance Enhancement

Optimize athletic performance through biomechanical assessment, movement analysis, and targeted treatment plans.
